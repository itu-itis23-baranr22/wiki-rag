2 · CAPPADOCIAPassage 2/43

Cappadocia is a historical region in Central Anatolia, Turkey. It is largely in the provinces of Nevşehir, Kayseri, Aksaray, Kırşehir, Sivas and Niğde. The name was traditionally used in Christian sources throughout history and is still widely used as an international tourism concept.
